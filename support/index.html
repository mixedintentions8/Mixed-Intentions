Stock your bar

In My Bar, use Add to select ingredients, including nonalcoholic mixers, juices and syrups. Use the camera button to photograph a bottle or select an existing photo. Check the full expression, age and size before confirming the bottle. Saving a bottle also adds its ingredient for cocktail matching.

Find your next cocktail

Matchmaker shows cocktails that are ready to make and cocktails that are one ingredient away. The Menu contains the recipe collection and favorites. Open a recipe to see its ingredients and directions; use an empty ingredient circle to add that ingredient to My Bar.

Understand estimated bar value

My Bar shows an estimated value for saved bottles with usable USD prices. It uses applicable size-matched online or published reference prices, or a purchase price you enter. It is a dated full-bottle estimate, not a measurement of remaining liquid or a guarantee of current local retail value. Tap the value to see its coverage and how it is calculated.

If a bottle is not recognized

Use good light, reduce glare and keep the brand, expression, age and size visible. Review the local match. If the expression differs or is missing, correct the bottle name and choose the deeper online search. Results stay in the app for review. You can also enter details manually and leave the price blank until verified. Catalog coverage varies; a brand match alone does not establish an expression or price.

The photo index primarily covers alcoholic bottles. Nonalcoholic mixers can be added through the ingredient picker even when their branded package is not recognized.

Custom recipes and House Pours

Add a custom recipe, choose its drink illustration and ingredients, and save it locally. House Pour lets you begin with a cocktail, spirit or ingredient and refine the balance. Sharing includes the selected recipe; private tasting notes and history stay local.

Keep your data

Your bar, favorites and saved recipes are stored locally and are designed to survive normal app updates. Avoid deleting the app when troubleshooting: deleting it may remove local data. The app does not provide account-based cloud synchronization. See your Apple device backup settings for device-level backup and restore options.

Contact us

Include the app version, device model, operating system version and steps that led to the issue. For recognition problems, include the brand, full expression and size, and the words the app read. You may attach a label photo if you choose. Please do not send passwords or payment information.
