# Mixed Intentions

Stock your bar. Know its value. Find your next pour.

Mixed Intentions (formerly Last Call) is a cocktail app for iPhone and iPad. Keep track of ingredients and bottles, scan labels, see an estimated bar value, find cocktails you can make, save favorites, and create your own recipes and House Pours.
