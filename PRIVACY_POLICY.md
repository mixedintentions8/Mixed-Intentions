# Mixed Intentions Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 15, 2026**

Mixed Intentions (formerly Last Call) helps you keep a home-bar inventory, discover and save cocktail recipes, scan bottle labels, and create your own pours. Features vary by app version.

## Your bar stays on your device

My Bar ingredients, saved bottles and bottle photos, favorites, custom recipes, House Pours, and tasting notes are saved locally in the app's storage. The app does not require an account and does not provide its own cloud synchronization service. We do not operate a server that receives a copy of your inventory or recipes.

Normal app updates are designed to retain this local data. Deleting the app or losing a device can remove local data. Device backups and restores are controlled by your Apple settings; they are not a separate backup service provided by us.

## Camera, photos, and label recognition

Camera access is requested when you choose to photograph a bottle. You may instead select a photo using Apple's photo picker. Bottle-label text recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Bottle images are processed on your device and are not uploaded by the recognition or online bottle-search features. A saved bottle photo remains in the app's local storage until it is removed with its bottle or the app's data.

## Optional online bottle information

The bundled bottle index can be matched offline. The app may download and cache a public Oregon OLCC price book. That bulk catalog download does not include your photo, search words, or inventory.

If you choose a deeper online bottle search, the bottle name and search words you review may be sent to public product and review sources, including Tastings, Blackwell's Wines & Spirits, Bottle Barn, and Bitters & Bottles. The app may read matching public product pages to find the expression, bottle size, source, and price. These requests do not include the bottle photo, your complete bar, favorites, tasting history, or custom recipes.

Network requests necessarily expose normal connection information, such as an IP address, to the receiving service. Those third parties may handle request logs under their own privacy policies. Opening a source page in the app's browser also uses that website's own privacy practices and any cookies it sets.

## Sharing and importing recipes

When you choose to share a recipe, the app creates the selected recipe card, recipe file, or import link and passes it to the destination you select in Apple's sharing interface. The selected recipe can include its name, ingredients, instructions, glass, and garnish. Private tasting notes and version history are not included by this sharing feature. The recipient and the service you choose control what happens to the shared content afterward.

## Analytics, advertising, and accounts

The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your bar inventory or use it for advertising. Apple may provide diagnostics or aggregated App Store information according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

## Support and public pages

If you email support, we receive the information and attachments you choose to send and use them to answer your request. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or other information we do not need. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve and follow up on your request.

Our public documentation is hosted by GitHub. Visiting those pages is subject to [GitHub's privacy practices](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Your choices

You can decline camera access, choose photos yourself, add ingredients manually, use the bundled catalog offline, and decide whether to run an online search or share a recipe. You can edit or remove saved items in the app. Removing the app removes its local app storage, subject to Apple's backup and restore behavior.

## Age suitability

The app is intended for adults of legal drinking age in their location. It is not directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's features or information practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
